HOW FLOOD FINDER WORKS

Built for coordination.

Know what the platform does—and when to call emergency services.

01

Create an account

Register with an email address and strong password. Your session is stored securely on the server. Accounts start as community reporters.

02

Pin an incident

Click the location on the report map or use device location with permission. Describe conditions, category, and urgency. Do not post private information.

03

Review and assign

Authorized administrators change verification status, create response crews, choose a lead, and assign teams to real incident coordinates.

04

Update response progress

Signed-in team leads see their assignments, mark steps of their response, and return completed incidents to the shared map.

Important safety notice

Flood Finder is not 911, an emergency dispatch authority, an evacuation directive, or a flood prediction system. For immediate danger, call local emergency services. Never drive through floodwater or rely solely on community reports or estimated routes.
